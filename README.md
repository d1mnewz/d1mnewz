## 🐾 Dzyga's Paw

In February 2022 I quit IT and started buying gear for friends at the front. People on [Twitter](https://x.com/dim0kq) started chipping in, and it grew into [Dzyga's Paw](https://dzygaspaw.com): $7M+ of tech delivered to 100+ units, donors from 70+ countries, [every dollar public](https://dzygaspaw.com/transparency).

Named after our dog Dzyga - well, her paw 🐕
